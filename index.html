CAPABILITY 02

Travel Security Services

From route planning to convoy escort, we manage the logistics of moving safely through unfamiliar terrain.

Planning for every turn

  • Trip risk assessment — route mapping, location-level risk analysis, and emergency contact points, culminating in a clear overall risk rating.
  • Arrival & departure assistance — coordinated logistics with local authorities and transport.
  • Security briefings — delivered online or on arrival, so your team knows exactly what to expect.
  • Expert route planning — built on deep, current knowledge of African terrain and conditions.
  • Vehicle & driver management — including armored options and security-trained drivers.

Added protection for high-value assets

  • Route & security planning — including Plan B contingency routes and designated safe zones.
  • Close protection officers — licensed, discreet, and trained for high-risk movement.
  • Medical response coordination — rapid assistance and evacuation by road or air if required.
  • Dedicated liaison officer — your on-the-ground point of contact, fluent in English or French.

Never lose track of your team

  • Multi-layered tracking — real-time device and platform monitoring of vehicles and personnel.
  • Secure communication protocols — clear, tested coordination between your team and ours.
  • Emergency response services — accident management, medical support, and rapid escalation when it matters.
